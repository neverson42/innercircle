InnerCircle

Your friends. Your groups. Your follows. Your control.

InnerCircle is a privacy-focused tool that puts you in control of your Facebook feed by letting you decide which people, groups, and accounts you see.

Getting started: Click Build Database below to collect your Facebook friends, groups, and followed accounts. Once collection is complete, InnerCircle can use that information to identify trusted and untrusted posts.

Local Database

InnerCircle opens three new tabs to collect your Facebook Friends, Groups, and Following lists. It scrolls through each list automatically and closes its collection tabs when finished. (Tab closes 10 seconds after scrolling stops to ensure any delayed elements are captured.) Collection can take up to 10 minutes, depending on the size of your lists.

Please don't use the browser until collection finishes.

Your database is stored locally on this device. Rebuild it whenever you add or remove friends, join or leave groups, or follow or unfollow accounts. If you do not rebuild the database, posts from new friends, follows, and groups will be hidden.

Test Mode

Show testing highlights

Trusted posts have green outlines. Untrusted posts have red outlines and Hide buttons. Posts are not automatically removed while Test Mode is ON.

When Test Mode is OFF, untrusted posts are removed automatically for a cleaner feed. Use Test Mode to check how posts are classified before relying on automatic filtering.
